# Using LLMs for personal finance safely — Explainer Book

> **You'll come away knowing:** How to use an AI assistant for budgeting, explanations, and planning while protecting your information. You will practice a workflow that keeps financial decisions and payments under your control.
> **For:** Beginners managing their own finances · **Total reading time:** About 10 minutes

## Why this matters

Money management involves repetitive work: organizing spending, anticipating bills, and comparing possible plans. A large language model, or **LLM**, is an AI system that generates text from patterns learned during training. You interact with it through requests called **prompts**.

Think of it as a drafting assistant beside your financial records. You supply the relevant facts, check its work, and decide what to do.

## The 60-second version

- Ask it to explain terms, suggest budget categories, compare hypothetical plans, and prepare questions for your bank.
- Share fictional numbers or minimal category totals. Keep credentials and full financial records private.
- Verify calculations with a calculator or spreadsheet, and consequential claims against official sources.
- Keep control of transfers, trades, borrowing, and filings. Check recipients and amounts in trusted financial channels.
- Pause urgent money requests and promises of guaranteed returns. Verify the sender independently.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain-English definition |
| --- | --- |
| LLM | AI system that generates text from learned patterns |
| Prompt | Your request to an AI assistant |
| Hallucination | A plausible but incorrect or invented answer |
| Redaction | Removing sensitive details before sharing |
| Retention / training use | How long data stays / whether it helps improve models |
| Memory | Saved details used in later conversations |
| Connector / read-only access | A service link / permission to view without changing |
| Multi-factor authentication | Additional proof of identity beyond a password |
| Local model | A model running on your device |
| Budget / cash flow | Planned allocations / timing of money coming and going |
| Sinking fund | Money saved gradually for a known future expense |
| Emergency savings | Money reserved for unexpected essential costs or income loss |
| Prompt injection | Malicious content that tries to redirect an assistant |

## Scope and assumptions

You need no technical background. Examples use fictional rupee amounts; the workflow applies across currencies. No country, income level, or investment objective is assumed.

This book provides education and a process for checking work, rather than a personal investment, tax, or debt prescription. **Hallucinations** are plausible but incorrect answers: the [UK National Cyber Security Centre](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/ai-and-cyber-security-what-you-need-to-know) identifies them as an LLM limitation.

As of November 2026, product privacy controls and financial rules require checking against current documentation. Recheck provider policies, official rules, and product terms when you use them. Sources are linked beside the claims they support.
